## About Me

I am an undergraduate student at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics and Physics and a B.Eng. in Software Engineering. I currently have a GPA of **4.0/4.0** and rank **1st out of 49** students in my cohort. In Fall 2026, I am joining the School of Computer Science at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/) as an exchange student.

My research interests lie in **robotics, embodied AI, generative models, and world models**. I am particularly interested in how intelligent systems can learn structured representations from human demonstrations and partial observations, then use them for robust prediction and action.

I have worked with Prof. Huazhe Xu at the Tsinghua Embodied AI Lab and am currently working with Prof. Ziming Liu at LIU Lab. Before college, I participated in mathematical olympiads and received a silver medal in the Chinese Mathematical Olympiad.

My curriculum vitae is available [here]({{ '/assets/files/academic_cv_siheng_xia.pdf' | relative_url }}). You can also find more details on my [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) and [experience]({{ '/experience/' | relative_url }}) pages.
