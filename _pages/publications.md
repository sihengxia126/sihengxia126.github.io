    <h2>DexReprise: Reference-Guided Generalist Dexterous Manipulation from Human Motion and Videos</h2>
    <p class="authors">Tianming Wei, Yufeng Tian, Jinxian Zhou, <strong>Siheng Xia</strong>, Zhecheng Yuan, and Huazhe Xu.</p>
    <p><em>Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026, under review.</em></p>
    <p>DexReprise builds closed-loop dexterous manipulation policies from offline human motion and video references, reducing dependence on real-time teleoperation. My contribution centered on spatial-awareness objectives for preserving 3D structure during policy distillation.</p>
